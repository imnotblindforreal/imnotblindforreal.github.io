I. Essay 1: Digital Technology in Education

Bấm vào các từ gạch chân đét-sốt để xem giải nghĩa chi tiết.

In recent years, digital technology has transformed traditional educational methods across the globe. The integration of laptops, tablets, and interactive software into classrooms has changed how students access information and collaborate with their peers. While some educators argue that technology can cause distractions, research suggests that when used effectively, digital tools enhance student engagement and academic performance.

One major advantage of digital learning is personalized education. Adaptive learning platforms analyze a student's strengths and weaknesses, allowing them to learn at their own pace. For instance, if a student struggles with a specific mathematical concept, the software provides additional exercises and explanations tailored to their needs. Furthermore, online resources give students immediate access to a vast array of research materials, enabling them to conduct independent study beyond standard textbooks.

However, the widespread use of technology in schools also presents significant challenges. The digital divide—the gap between those who have access to modern technology and those who do not—remains a serious issue in many developing regions. Without equal access to devices and reliable internet connection, disadvantaged students risk falling behind. Therefore, educational institutions and governments must work together to ensure that technological advancements benefit all learners, regardless of their socioeconomic background.

II. Essay 2: Artificial Intelligence in Daily Life

Bấm vào các từ gạch chân đét-sốt để xem giải nghĩa chi tiết.

Artificial intelligence (AI) has rapidly progressed from a theoretical concept to an integral component of daily routines. From smartphone voice assistants to automated recommendation systems on streaming platforms, AI algorithms analyze vast datasets to predict consumer behavior and streamline complex tasks. In healthcare, AI-driven diagnostic tools assist medical professionals by analyzing medical images with high speed and precision, leading to earlier detection of diseases.

Despite these remarkable advancements, the ethical implications of AI remain a subject of intense debate among researchers and policymakers. A primary concern involves data privacy, as these systems rely heavily on personal information to operate effectively. Additionally, automation raises economic questions regarding potential job displacement in traditional industries. As AI technology continues to evolve, establishing clear ethical guidelines is essential to ensure that automated systems are safe, fair, and beneficial for society as a whole.

III. Bài đọc mẫu 3: Renewable Energy Technologies

Chủ đề: Sustainable Energy & Innovations. Bấm vào các từ gạch chân để xem giải nghĩa chi tiết.

The global transition toward sustainable energy sources has accelerated development in renewable energy technologies. Innovations in photovoltaic solar panels and wind turbine design have significantly increased the efficiency of clean energy production while lowering manufacturing costs. Furthermore, modern smart grid technology enables power companies to manage energy distribution more effectively by monitoring demand in real time.

However, one of the main technical hurdles facing renewable energy is energy storage. Because solar and wind power depend on variable weather conditions, energy generation is inherently unpredictable. Engineers are actively developing next-generation battery technology, such as solid-state and lithium-sulfur batteries, to store surplus energy generated during peak hours for later use. Overcoming these storage limitations is critical for building a resilient energy infrastructure capable of replacing fossil fuels entirely.

IV. Bài đọc mẫu 4: The Internet of Things & Cybersecurity

Chủ đề: IoT, Digital Security & Risks. Bấm vào các từ gạch chân để xem giải nghĩa chi tiết.

The rapid expansion of the Internet of Things (IoT)—a network of physical objects connected to the internet—has changed how we interact with technology. Today, smart appliances, fitness trackers, and industrial sensors collect and share data continuously. This high level of connectivity offers great convenience by automating daily tasks and improving operational efficiency across various industries.

However, the growth of connected devices also introduces serious cybersecurity risks. Many low-cost IoT devices are manufactured with minimal security features and rarely receive software updates. Consequently, cybercriminals can exploit these vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access to private networks or steal personal data. In extreme cases, hackers can take control of thousands of compromised devices to launch coordinated attacks against critical infrastructure, such as power grids or banking systems.

Protecting these systems requires a proactive approach from both developers and users. Manufacturers must integrate strong security protocols during the design phase, while users should adopt safe practices like creating strong passwords and enabling multi-factor authentication. As reliance on smart technology continues to grow, maintaining strong digital security will remain a top priority.

V. Bài đọc mẫu 5: Renewable Energy & Storage Challenges

Chủ đề: Sustainable Energy, Grid Systems & Storage. Bấm vào các từ gạch chân để xem giải nghĩa chi tiết.

The global shift toward sustainable energy has driven remarkable progress in renewable energy technology. Improvements in solar panel efficiency and wind turbine design have significantly lowered the cost of generating clean power. Additionally, smart grid systems allow energy companies to track electricity demand in real time and distribute power more efficiently to homes and businesses.

Despite these advancements, storing renewable energy remains a major technical challenge. Solar and wind energy are dependent on weather conditions, which makes power production unpredictable. During periods of low sunlight or light winds, energy output drops significantly. To address this issue, engineers are developing advanced battery technologies that can store large amounts of surplus electricity produced during peak hours for later use.

Overcoming these storage limitations is essential for creating a reliable energy grid. As battery technology improves, renewable energy will play an increasingly vital role in replacing traditional fossil fuels and reducing global carbon emissions.
